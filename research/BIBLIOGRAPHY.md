# MELFINA — RESEARCH BIBLIOGRAPHY

References for `RESEARCH_MASTER.md` (RESEARCH MISSION 001).

**Provenance notes.** This was a scoping search (Sept 2026), not a systematic
review. Entries are marked:

- **(verified)** — abstract or full text or a direct systematic summary was read
  this pass.
- **(abstract/summary)** — only an abstract, database snippet, or reputable
  secondary summary was read; effect sizes should be re-checked against the primary
  source before requirements.
- **(background)** — well-established work cited from prior knowledge, not
  re-fetched this pass; listed for completeness and to be confirmed later.

## ADHD — cognition and mechanisms

- **Alderson, R. M., Kasper, L. J., Hudec, K. L., & Patros, C. H. G. (2013).**
  Attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder (ADHD) and working memory in adults: a
  meta-analytic review. *Neuropsychology, 27*(3), 287–302. — Verbal WM *g* ≈ 0.55,
  visuospatial *g* ≈ 0.49; deficits persist into adulthood; larger under
  central-executive load. (abstract/summary) PMID 23688211.
- **Barkley, R. A. (1997).** Behavioral inhibition, sustained attention, and
  executive functions: constructing a unifying theory of ADHD. *Psychological
  Bulletin, 121*(1), 65–94. — Inhibition-centred EF/self-regulation model.
  (background)
- **Barkley, R. A. (1997 / later).** ADHD and the nature of self-control; and
  "point of performance" intervention argument. (background)
- **Zheng, Q., Wang, X., Chiu, K. Y., & Shum, K. K.-M. (2022).** Time perception
  deficits in children and adolescents with ADHD: a meta-analysis. *Journal of
  Attention Disorders, 26*(2), 267–281. — 27 studies; accuracy *g* > 0.40,
  precision *g* = 0.66; visual & auditory. (abstract/summary)
- **Nejati, V., & Yazdani, S. (various).** Time discrimination meta-analytic
  findings in ADHD (~25 studies, ~1,633 participants; medium effect). Cited via
  adhdevidence.org synthesis "Time blindness found to be a consistent feature of
  ADHD." (summary)
- **Jackson, J. N. S., & MacKillop, J. (2016).** Attention-deficit/hyperactivity
  disorder and monetary delay discounting: a meta-analysis of case-control
  studies. *Biological Psychiatry: CNNI, 1*(4), 316–325. — Steeper discounting in
  ADHD. (abstract/summary)
- **Marx, I., Hacker, T., Yu, X., Cortese, S., & Sonuga-Barke, E. (2021).** ADHD
  and the choice of small immediate over larger delayed rewards: a comparative
  meta-analysis... *Journal of Attention Disorders, 25*(2), 171–187. — 37 group
  comparisons, 3,763 participants; small-to-medium effects; real rewards/real
  waiting sharpen it. (abstract/summary)
- **Sonuga-Barke, E. J. S. (2002/2003).** The dual pathway model of ADHD:
  executive dysfunction + delay aversion (motivational pathway). (background +
  summary)
- **Talbot, K. D. S., & Kerns, K. A. (2014).** Event- and time-based prospective
  memory in children with ADHD (and related reviews). — Time-based PM reliably
  impaired; event-based inconsistent; cueing helps. (abstract/summary; PMID
  29065807 review)
- **Beheshti, A., Chavanon, M.-L., & Christiansen, H. (2020).** Emotion
  dysregulation in adults with ADHD: a systematic review. *BMC Psychiatry, 20*,
  120. — Emotion dysregulation as a core (not incidental) feature; strong link to
  psychosocial impairment. (abstract/summary; PMC9821724 related)
- **"The lived experience of rejection sensitivity in ADHD — a qualitative
  exploration." (2024/2026).** *PLOS ONE.* — Functional impact of
  rejection-sensitivity experiences; note "RSD" is not a formal diagnostic
  construct. (verified — abstract/printable)

## ADHD — intervention

- **NICE (2018, updated).** Attention deficit hyperactivity disorder: diagnosis
  and management. NG87. nice.org.uk/guidance/ng87. — Combined psychoeducation +
  CBT + environmental/organisational strategies + medication where appropriate.
  (verified — overview)
- **Meta-analyses of CBT for adult ADHD** (multiple; syntheses at
  adhdevidence.org, and e.g. *Journal of Affective Disorders* 2025 meta-analysis).
  — Medium-to-large within-group, small-to-medium between-group symptom
  reductions; functional gains in time management/organisation. (summary)
- **Lauder, K., McDowall, A., & Tenenbaum, H. R. (2024).** Meta-analysis of
  pharmacological and psychosocial interventions for work-relevant outcomes in
  adults with ADHD. (abstract/summary)

## Autism

- **Demetriou, E. A., Lampit, A., Quintana, D. S., et al. (2018).** Autism
  spectrum disorders: a meta-analysis of executive function. *Molecular
  Psychiatry, 23*, 1198–1204. — Medium, domain-general EF effect (~*g* 0.5); no
  clean fractionation; relatively stable across the lifespan; limited diagnostic
  utility. (abstract/summary — full text paywalled) PMID 28439105.
- **"Autistic traits are associated with lower perceived executive function but
  not poorer executive function task performance..." (2025).** *Molecular Autism,
  16*. — Perceived vs measured EF divergence. (verified — abstract)
- **Yeung, M. K., et al. (2024).** Longitudinal changes in executive function in
  ASD: systematic review and meta-analyses. *Autism Research.* (abstract/summary)
- **Murray, D., Lesser, M., & Lawson, W. (2005).** Attention, monotropism and the
  diagnostic criteria for autism. *Autism, 9*(2), 139–156. — Monotropism theory.
  (verified — summary; monotropism.org)
- **Garau, V., et al. (2023).** The Monotropism Questionnaire (47-item). — Traits
  correlate with autistic & ADHD traits; not diagnostic. (summary)
- **"Investigating autistic hyperfocus and monotropism: limited convergence of
  ERPs, lab tasks, and questionnaire responses." (2025).** — Construct better
  supported experientially than neurophysiologically. (verified — abstract)
- **Jenkinson, R., Milne, E., & Thompson, A. (2020).** The relationship between
  intolerance of uncertainty and anxiety in autism: a systematic review and
  meta-analysis. *Autism, 24*(8), 1933–1944. — IU↔anxiety *r* ≈ 0.62. (verified —
  abstract; PMC7539603)
- **(2024) systematic review and meta-analysis:** restrictive/repetitive
  behaviours, intolerance of uncertainty, and anxiety in autism — 33 papers,
  8,347 participants; mutually correlated triad. *Research in Autism / ScienceDirect
  S175094672400103X.* (abstract/summary)
- **Ben-Sasson, A., Hen, L., Fluss, R., Cermak, S. A., Engel-Yeger, B., & Gal, E.
  (2009).** A meta-analysis of sensory modulation symptoms in individuals with
  ASD. *Journal of Autism and Developmental Disorders, 39*, 1–11. — Large group
  differences; under-responsivity > over-responsivity > seeking; moderated by age
  and comparison group. (verified — abstract; PMID 18512135)
- **Milton, D. E. M. (2012).** On the ontological status of autism: the "double
  empathy problem." *Disability & Society, 27*(6), 883–887. (verified — KAR PDF
  reference)
- **Milton, D., Gurbuz, E., & López, B. (2022).** The "double empathy problem":
  ten years on. *Autism, 26*(8). (verified — abstract)
- **Crompton, C. J., et al. (2020).** Information transfer within and between
  autistic and non-autistic people (diffusion-chain study). *Autism.* (background
  + summary)
- **Raymaker, D. M., et al. (2020).** "Having all of your internal resources
  exhausted beyond measure...": defining autistic burnout. *Autism in Adulthood,
  2*(2), 132–143. AASPIRE. — 3+ months exhaustion, loss of function, reduced
  stimulus tolerance; expectations–abilities mismatch without support. (verified —
  summary + PDF; PMID 32851204)
- **Cognitive flexibility / real-world flexibility and anxiety in autistic youth.
  (2025).** *Frontiers in Psychiatry.* — Real-world (report) flexibility predicts
  anxiety; lab task-switching often does not. (verified — abstract)
- **Pathological Demand Avoidance / "Persistent Drive for Autonomy":** overviews —
  National Autistic Society (autism.org.uk); Child Mind Institute; *The
  Transmitter* (2024) "PDA in autism, explained." — Intrinsic need for autonomy;
  autonomy-supportive approaches reduce demand response. (verified — overviews;
  construct is contested/not in DSM-5)
- **Self-Determination Theory:** Deci, E. L., & Ryan, R. M. (autonomous vs
  controlled motivation; autonomy support). (background)

## OCD

- **Rachman, S. (2002).** A cognitive theory of compulsive checking. *Behaviour
  Research and Therapy, 40*(6), 625–639. — Inflated responsibility × probability ×
  severity; self-perpetuating cycle; memory-confidence erosion. (verified —
  summary; PMID 12051482)
- **van den Hout, M., & Kindt, M. (2003).** Repeated checking causes memory
  distrust. *Behaviour Research and Therapy, 41*(3), 301–316. — Checking a virtual
  stove → less vivid/detailed recollection, lower confidence, unchanged accuracy;
  large effects. (verified — summary; PMID 12600401)
- **van den Hout, M., et al. (2010).** Repeated checking causes distrust in memory
  but not in attention and perception. *Behaviour Research and Therapy, 48*.
  (abstract/summary; PMID 20398897)
- **Giele, C. L., et al. (2015).** Repeated checking induces uncertainty about
  future threat. *Journal of Experimental Psychopathology.* (abstract/summary)
- **Coles, M. E., Frost, R. O., Heimberg, R. G., & Rhéaume, J. (2003).** "Not just
  right experiences": perfectionism, obsessive-compulsive features and general
  psychopathology. *Behaviour Research and Therapy, 41*(6), 681–700. (background +
  summary)
- **Sica, C., et al. (2022).** Not just right experiences and incompleteness as a
  predictor of OC symptoms in clinical and community samples: a meta-analysis.
  *Journal of Obsessive-Compulsive and Related Disorders, 34.* (verified —
  abstract; S2211364922000550)
- **Salkovskis, P. M. (1985).** Obsessional-compulsive problems: a cognitive-
  behavioural analysis. Inflated responsibility. (background)
- **Intolerance of Uncertainty as a cognitive vulnerability for OCD: a qualitative
  review.** *PMC11488751* (2024/earlier). — IU as candidate vulnerability; effect
  on treatment as mechanism still open. (verified — summary)
- **Network analyses of IU components × OCD symptoms.** *PMC12318506* (2025). —
  "Can't function when uncertain" ↔ "can't control my thoughts." (verified —
  summary)
- **Family accommodation of OCD across the lifespan.** *PMC12962741* (2024). —
  Accommodation (incl. reassurance provision) predicts greater severity at
  treatment end and follow-up. (verified — summary)
- **Lebowitz, E. R., et al.; Wu, M. S., et al.** Family accommodation in OCD —
  one of the strongest predictors of severity and poor outcome. (background +
  summary)
- **Inhibitory learning approach to ERP:** Craske, M. G., et al. (2014/2008);
  IOCDF expert opinion "The Inhibitory Learning Approach to ERP." — Exposure
  builds competing safety association; safety behaviours/avoidance make learning
  context-bound and non-generalising. (verified — IOCDF; background for Craske)
- **Blakey, S. M., & Abramowitz, J. S. (2016).** The effects of safety behaviors
  during exposure therapy for anxiety: critical analysis from an inhibitory
  learning perspective. *Clinical Psychology Review, 49*, 1–15. (verified — PDF
  reference)

## OCD / autism / ADHD overlap

- **"Obsessive–Compulsive Disorder and Autism Spectrum Disorder: clinical
  fingerprints of the comorbidity." (2026).** *Life, 16*(5), 792 (MDPI) /
  PMC13208157. — ~24.7% comorbid ASD in one OCD sample; distinct subgroup (earlier
  onset, symmetry/ordering/checking, somatic/religious obsessions). (verified —
  summary)
- **"Repetitive Behaviors in Autism and Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder: A

  anxiety, EF, sensory processing, not by topography; ego-dystonic vs
  self-affirming distinction. (verified — summary)

  *Journal of Psychiatric Research* S0022395625002961. (abstract/summary)

  Occur Together"; *The Conversation* (2024) "What is AuDHD?"; Vanderbilt Frist
  Center. — High co-occurrence; structure-vs-novelty and overwhelm-vs-restlessness
  tensions; "flexible routines / anchor points" suggested. (verified — overviews;
  the 40% figure is commonly cited, sample-dependent)
- **Inhibition, flexibility, working memory and planning in ASD ± comorbid ADHD
  symptoms.** *PMC2276205.* (abstract/summary)

## Cognitive science — memory, offloading, interruption, time, planning

- **Risko, E. F., & Gilbert, S. J. (2016).** Cognitive offloading. *Trends in
  Cognitive Sciences, 20*(9), 676–688. — Definition; three-component metacognitive
  framework. (verified — summary; PMID 27542527)
- **Gilbert, S. J., et al. (2022).** Outsourcing memory to external tools: a
  review of "intention offloading." *Psychonomic Bulletin & Review* (samgilbert.net
  preprint). — Offloading improves target-task performance; decision to offload is
  biased and metacognition-linked; special populations benefit but mis-calibrate;
  tool trust moderates. (search-summary — PDF fetch failed on cert)
- **Zeigarnik, B. (1927).** Das Behalten erledigter und unerledigter Handlungen.
  *Psychologische Forschung, 9*, 1–85. — Unfinished tasks stay active.
  (background)
- **Masicampo, E. J., & Baumeister, R. F. (2011).** Consider it done! Plan-making
  can eliminate the cognitive effects of unfulfilled goals. *Journal of
  Personality and Social Psychology, 101*(4), 667–683. — Unfinished goals →
  intrusive thoughts + impaired subsequent focus; a *specific plan* removes the
  effect. (verified — summary)
- **Altmann, E. M., & Trafton, J. G. (2002).** Memory for goals: an
  activation-based model. *Cognitive Science, 26*(1), 39–83. (background +
  summary)
- **Trafton, J. G., Altmann, E. M., & Brock, D. P. (2005); Altmann (2004),
  "Task interruption: resumption lag and the role of cues."** — Resumption lag;
  cues + goal rehearsal mitigate. (verified — interruptions.net PDFs referenced)
- **Monk, C. A., Trafton, J. G., & Boehm-Davis, D. A. (2008).** The effect of
  interruption duration and demand on resuming suspended goals. *Journal of
  Experimental Psychology: Applied, 14*(4), 299–313. — Longer/more demanding
  interruptions → longer resumption lag. (verified — JEP:A PDF reference; PMID
  19102614)
- **Buehler, R., Griffin, D., & Ross, M. (1994).** Exploring the "planning
  fallacy": why people underestimate their task completion times. *Journal of
  Personality and Social Psychology, 67*(3), 366–381. — Thesis prediction 33.9d vs
  actual 55.5d; bias specific to own tasks. (verified — MIT-hosted PDF)
- **Kahneman, D., & Tversky, A. (1979).** Origin of the term "planning fallacy."
  (background)
- **Gollwitzer, P. M., & Sheeran, P. (2006).** Implementation intentions and goal
  achievement: a meta-analysis of effects and processes. *Advances in Experimental
  Social Psychology, 38*, 69–119. — *d* = 0.65 on attainment (94 studies, 8,000+
  participants); *d* = 0.77 against derailment. (verified — summary)
- **Choice overload / paradox of choice:** Iyengar, S. S., & Lepper, M. R.
  (2000); Scheibehenne, Greifeneder & Todd (2010, meta-analysis showing the
  effect is real but moderated). (background)

## HCI / accessibility / notifications / adaptation

- **W3C / WAI (2021, updated).** Making Content Usable for People with Cognitive

- **Iqbal, S. T., & Bailey, B. P. (2008).** Effects of intelligent notification

  distraction *and* more anxiety/disconnection. (verified — reference)
- **Alert fatigue / clinical decision support:** Ancker, J. S., et al. (2017),
  "Effects of workload, work complexity, and repeated alerts on alert fatigue,"
  *BMC Medical Informatics and Decision Making* — responsiveness drops with
  repetition (~30%/repeat commonly cited). (background + summary)
- **Findlater, L., & McGrenere, J. (2004).** A comparison of static, adaptive, and
  adaptable menus. *CHI 2004.* — Static fastest, adaptable next, adaptive slowest;
  users prefer adaptable (control/predictability). (verified — summary; ACM PDF)
- **Findlater, L., & McGrenere, J. (2008).** Impact of screen size on performance,
  awareness, and user satisfaction with adaptive graphical user interfaces.
  (background)
- **Gajos, K. Z., et al. (2009).** "Ephemeral adaptation." *CHI 2009.* —
  Highlight-don't-move mitigates adaptive-UI cost. (background + summary)
- **Heylighen, F., & Vidal, C. (2008).** Getting Things Done: the science behind
  stress-free productivity. *Long Range Planning, 41*(6), 585–605. — Situated/
  embodied/distributed cognition account of GTD; notes absence of controlled
  outcome evidence. (verified — summary; cogprints PDF)
- **Sohn, T., et al. (2005).** Place-Its: a study of location-based reminders on
  mobile phones. *UbiComp 2005.* And later work "Exploring the role of prospective
  memory in location-based reminders" (UbiComp 2014). — Location reminders used but
  "still not an effective tool"; triggering imprecision. (verified — references)
- **Body doubling in HCI:** Eagle, T., et al. (CHI 2023) framing body doubling as
  a continuum of space/time/mutuality; arXiv roadmap for mixed-reality body
  doubling (2026). — Active topic; limited outcome evidence. (search-summary)
- **HCI + ADHD:** "ADHD and Technology Research – Investigated by Neurodivergent
  Readers" (*CHI 2022*); "ADHD and Knowledge Work: ... Opportunities for AI"
